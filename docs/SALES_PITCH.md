# CounterIQ — Sales Pitch

## Positioning (one sentence)
"AI-assisted loss prevention and store operations monitoring for high-risk
retail. Existing cameras. One email every morning. Privacy-first."

## Owner-to-owner one-liner
"CounterIQ doesn't watch people — it watches the patterns that cost you
money. Possible product-loss exits, unmatched register events, register
left empty, after-hours motion. Every morning you get a one-page email.
You make the call."

## Pricing positioning
"We don't charge a setup fee. CounterIQ starts at $299/month and includes
up to 4 cameras. If the store needs more camera coverage, we scale the
plan based on the number of cameras and features needed."

## Value statement
"If CounterIQ helps you catch one missed transaction, one product-loss
event, or one off-register cash exchange, it can pay for itself that
month."

## 30-second walk-up
"Hey, you the owner? Real quick — not selling cameras. I built software
that watches the cameras you already have and emails you a one-page
report every morning. Register left empty, after-hours motion, anything
that looked off, with a clip attached. No facial recognition, no audio
recording by default. Starts at $299 a month, includes up to 4 cameras,
no setup fee. First 10 stores lock that rate for a year. Got 5 minutes?"

## 5-minute demo (rehearsed)
[0:00] Hook — "When's the last time you scrubbed your camera footage?"
[0:30] What it does — "Plugs into existing cameras over RTSP. Small box
       runs in the back. Watches 24/7. Flags 7 things on video. One-page
       email every morning at 6am."
[1:30] Live demo — yesterday's email on tablet. 142 visits, register
       empty 14 min, clip plays in 2 seconds.
[3:00] Why your shop — "Catches register-empty during peak, after-hours
       motion, possible product-loss exits, unmatched cash/product
       exchanges once POS connects. If it catches one $200 off-register
       exchange a month, it's paid for itself for the year."
[3:45] Pricing + privacy — "$299/month, up to 4 cameras, no setup fee.
       No facial recognition. No transcription. Audio off by default."
[4:15] Pilot offer — "First 10 stores lock $299 for a full 12 months.
       Cancel anytime after 60 days. We help install and set up zones."
[4:45] Close — "Anything happening at the store you wish you could see
       without scrubbing? Can I install Tuesday or Wednesday?"

## Strongest sales angle
"Your cameras already record everything. They don't tell you anything.
CounterIQ tells you. One email. Every morning. Without facial
recognition, without listening to anyone. The clip is the evidence —
we just help you find it."

## Lead with these features in this order
1. Daily 1-page email at 6am.
2. Register-empty / counter-unattended detection.
3. After-hours motion alerts.
4. Privacy-first (no FR, no STT, no demographic).
5. Bring-your-own-camera (no NVR replacement).
6. No setup fee — $299/month and you're running.
7. Lock the rate for 12 months as one of the first 10 stores.

## Don't lead with
- "AI." Owners hear hype.
- "Computer vision." Sounds expensive.
- "Theft detection." Triggers legal nervousness.
- "Employee monitoring." Triggers HR nervousness.
- POS integration unless they ask (it's the upsell).

## Tier upgrade path (when they ask "what about more features?")
- Starter $299 (4 cams) → Pro $399 (6 cams + product-loss + cash-exchange
  review) → Advanced $499 (8 cams + longer retention + priority support).
- Add-ons later: POS integration +$99, audio events +$49, extended
  retention +$49, extra cameras $25–$39 each.
