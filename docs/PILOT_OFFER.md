# CounterIQ — Paid Pilot Offer

This is a paid pilot. Not a free trial. We earn the relationship by
delivering value from month one — not by giving away the product.

## Offer (first 10 stores)
- **No setup fee.**
- **Starts at $299/month** (Starter tier, up to 4 cameras).
- **Cancel anytime after 60 days.**
- **First 10 stores lock $299/month for 12 months** — no price increase
  during that window even when standard pricing rises.
- CounterIQ helps configure cameras and zones during onboarding.
- One edge device included (loaned for the duration).

## Why a paid pilot beats a free trial
- Owner has skin in the game. Engagement is real, not polite.
- We learn what's actually worth $299 in their world.
- Sets the price expectation from day 1 — no painful conversion later.
- Filters out tire-kickers. Saves your time.

## What's included at $299 (Starter)
- Up to 4 cameras
- Daily AI report at 6am
- Incident clips with 30-day retention
- Counter-unattended detection
- After-hours motion alerts
- Restricted-zone entry alerts
- SMS + email alerts
- Privacy-first audit log
- Privacy & audio settings (audio OFF by default)

## Upgrade options during pilot
- **Pro — $399/month**: 6 cameras + possible product-loss exit + unmatched
  cash/product exchange + multi-clip package viewer.
- **Advanced — $499/month**: 8 cameras + longer retention + priority
  support + advanced reporting.
- Add-ons: extra camera $25–$39, POS integration +$99, audio events +$49,
  extended retention +$49.

## Targeting (first 10)
Within 30 miles. Mix:
- 3 vape / smoke shops
- 4 gas stations / c-stores
- 2 liquor stores
- 1 wildcard (specialty retail, e.g. dispensary, beauty supply)

## What to measure (non-negotiable)
- Login frequency (active use signal)
- # daily reports opened
- # incidents marked Important / Resolved
- # alerts sent vs # owner found useful (FP rate)
- # uptime hours / 1440
- NPS at day 30 and day 60
- Owner-stated $ value: "What did this catch this month?"

## What makes a pilot successful
- Owner opens email ≥5 of 7 mornings in week 4.
- ≥1 incident the owner says "I would not have known about this."
- Owner stays past day 60 (no cancellation).
- Provides written testimonial + 2 referrals by day 90.

## Day-30 testimonial ask (in person)
"If CounterIQ has been useful this month, would you record a 60-second
video on your phone — what you've used it for, what it caught? Easiest
way for me to land the next 5 stores. Mind if I ask?"

## Day-45 referral ask
"Real quick — give me 2 names. Other owners you know who'd want a 5-min
demo. I'll text once. If they ignore, I'm gone."

## Pricing-lock guarantee for first 10 (in writing)
"As one of the first 10 CounterIQ stores, your monthly rate is locked
for 12 months from your install date. Standard pricing may rise; yours
will not. If you upgrade tiers, the upgrade rate is also locked for the
remainder of the 12 months."
