# CounterIQ - Objection Handling

## "How much?"
$299/month for Starter - up to 4 cameras, daily report, alerts, 30-day
clip retention. No setup fee. First 10 pilot stores lock $299/month for
a full 12 months. Cancel anytime after 60 days.

## "Is there a free trial?"
No - CounterIQ is a paid product from day one. The trade-off is the
first-10 rate lock: $299/month guaranteed for 12 months, even when
standard pricing rises. If it's not useful after 60 days, you cancel,
no penalty.

## "Why no free trial?"
Two reasons. One - paid pilots get real engagement; free trials get
polite ignored. Two - I'd rather lock your rate at $299 for a year than
give you 60 days free and raise it on you later. Better deal for both
of us.

## "What if I need more cameras?"
Pro is $399/month for up to 6 cameras and adds possible product-loss
exit detection and unmatched cash/product exchange review. Advanced is
$499/month for up to 8 cameras with longer retention and priority
support. Above tier limits, extra cameras are $25-$39 each per month.

## "What about POS integration / audio?"
Both are add-ons. POS is +$99/month and unlocks unmatched-transaction
matching. Audio event detection - non-speech only, glass break, alarms,
after-hours sound - is +$49/month and requires a compliance flow.
Neither is needed at MVP for most stores.

## "Will my rate go up?"
Not for the first 10 stores. Your $299 is locked for 12 months from
install. Standard pricing may rise - yours doesn't.

## "What if I cancel?"
After 60 days you can cancel anytime. We pull the box, no charge. Your
data is exported or deleted, your choice.

## "My cameras already record everything."
Right - and how often do you scrub them? Recording isn't the problem.
Nobody watches. This emails you what mattered yesterday.

## "I don't trust putting a box on my network."
Fair. The box is hardware-isolated from your POS network - only reads
RTSP from your NVR, same as your phone app. I can show you the network
diagram. And if you want to pull the plug after a week, I come pick it up.

## "Is this legal? Can I monitor my employees?"
Yes - single-party-consent state, plus you'll post signage and we'll give
you an employee notice template. We don't do facial recognition or audio
by default. Owners are doing this everywhere; the difference is you'll
actually see what's in the footage.

## "My employees will quit."
The good ones won't. Most owners say their good employees love it because
it protects them from false claims. We don't do facial recognition or
transcription. We flag operational events for you to review - you make
all the decisions.

## "Send me info, I'll think about it."
Sure - but rate-lock spots are limited. There are [N] left in [city].
Easier path: let me drop off the box for a 24-hour test. You see
tomorrow's email Wednesday morning. If it's not useful, I pull it. Worst
case you lose 30 minutes.

## "I have to ask my partner / wife / boss."
Smart. What night this week could the three of us do a 10-minute call?
I'd rather show them than have you re-pitch from memory.

## "We tried something like this and it didn't work."
What was it? What didn't work? [Listen.] OK - here's what's different
about this: [pick one based on what they said: bring-your-own-camera,
plain-English daily report, no monthly $1500 hardware lock-in,
privacy-first].

## "What if my internet goes out?"
The box keeps recording locally. Events queue. When internet's back, it
syncs. You don't lose anything.

## "What if my cameras are old?"
If they output RTSP - and ~95% do - we work. Hikvision, Dahua, Reolink,
Lorex, Amcrest, Swann, Uniview. Even if they're 8 years old.

## "What about audio?"
Off by default. If you ever turn it on, there's a 5-step compliance flow
that walks you through your state's law and signage requirements. Even
when on, we don't transcribe speech - only detect non-speech security
sounds like glass break, alarms, after-hours sound.

## "How accurate is it?"
On counter-unattended detection, ~95% precision. We're conservative on
purpose. Better to miss something than send you a false alert at 2am.

## "What about facial recognition?"
We don't do it. Permanently. It's in our TOS and the dependency is
blocked at the build level. We watch behavior patterns, not identity.

## "Why should I trust you?"
You shouldn't yet. That's why the rate lock exists. $299 locked for a
year. If after 60 days you don't see value, you cancel. Worst case you
spent $600 on something you didn't like.

## "I'd rather hire a manager."
Sure - that's $4K+/month. CounterIQ is $299. Most owners use both: we do
the watching, the manager does the management.

## "What's the catch with the rate lock?"
No catch. The math is simple: I want 10 reference customers fast. You
get a 12-month guaranteed price. If we both still like each other in 12
months, we figure out renewal. If not, you cancel.
