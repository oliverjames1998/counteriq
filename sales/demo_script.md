# CounterIQ - 5-Minute Demo Script

Practice this until it sounds like a conversation, not a pitch.

## Setup
- Tablet with mock CounterIQ dashboard loaded.
- 90-second screen recording on standby (in case they're moving).
- 1-page printed leave-behind.
- Edge box in your bag (let them touch it).

---

## [0:00] OPENING (walk up to counter)

"Hey, you the owner? Cool. Real quick - I'm not selling cameras. I built
software that watches the cameras you already have and emails you a
one-page report every morning. I'm in your shop because I'm rolling out
my first 10 paid pilots in [city] and I'd rather have a real shop than a
demo. Got 5 minutes? I can show you on my tablet."

[Pause. Wait.]

---

## [0:30] PAIN HOOK

"Quick question first. When was the last time you actually scrubbed back
through your camera footage? Yeah - most owners say a month, two months,
never unless something goes missing. Problem is, by the time something's
missing, the cash is gone or the kid you sold a vape to is at the ER. I
built this because cameras shouldn't be a recording device. They should
be a manager."

---

## [1:00] WHAT IT DOES (30 sec)

"Plugs into your existing system - Hikvision, Dahua, Reolink, whatever
you've got. No new cameras, no NVR replacement. Small box runs in the
back. It watches 24/7 and flags 7 things on video: register empty more
than 5 minutes during business hours, anyone behind the counter, motion
after close, dwell at high-value walls, restricted-area entry, after-hours
sound if you turn audio on - but audio's off by default. Every morning at
6am you get one email."

---

## [1:30] LIVE DEMO (90 sec)

"Look - this is from a shop that's been running 8 days. Here's yesterday's
email."

[Open dashboard on tablet. Tap into Daily Report.]

"Top: 142 visits, peaked 4-6pm. Register was empty 14 minutes between
14:38 and 14:46 - here's the clip."

[Play 10s clip.]

"Restricted zone entry at 19:32 - clip."

[Play 5s.]

"After-hours motion at 02:14 - clip."

[Play 5s.]

"Three things this owner didn't know. Took me 60 seconds to read the
email."

---

## [3:00] WHY YOUR SHOP

"What this catches at a place like yours: register being unmanned during
peak, employees giving free product to friends, after-hours motion, and
once we plug in your POS, possible product-loss exits and unmatched
cash/product exchanges with no register match. The math is simple - if
it catches one $200 sweetheart transaction a month, it pays for itself
several times over."

---

## [3:30] PRIVACY ANSWER (before they ask)

"Couple things people always ask - no, we don't do facial recognition.
Don't transcribe what anyone says. Don't infer demographics or emotion.
Audio's off by default and there's a 5-step compliance flow if you ever
want to turn it on for things like glass-break or alarm detection. Every
clip view is logged. You're in full control."

---

## [4:00] PRICING

"$299/month for Starter - that's up to 4 cameras, daily report, alerts,
incident clips, 30-day retention. No setup fee. If you need more cameras
or want product-loss exit detection or POS-matched cash-exchange events,
Pro is $399 for up to 6 cameras and Advanced is $499 for up to 8."

## [4:15] RATE LOCK

"Here's the deal - you'd be one of my first 10 pilot stores. That means
$299 a month is locked for a full 12 months from your install date. Even
when standard pricing goes up, yours doesn't. Cancel anytime after the
first 60 days."

## [4:30] CLOSE

"Two questions.

One - anything happening at the store you wish you could see without
scrubbing footage?

Two - can I install Tuesday or Wednesday this week?"

[Pause. Shut up. Let them talk.]

---

## IF THEY HESITATE

"Couple options. One - sleep on it, I'll text you Friday with the agreement.
Two - we lock you into the first-10 rate today even if you start install
in two weeks. The 12-month lock is the move; that goes away after the
10th store signs."

---

## IF THEY SAY YES

"Awesome. I'll text you the 1-page pilot agreement tonight. Tuesday or
Wednesday next week - which works better? I'll be on-site for 30 min,
out of your way."

---

## IF THEY SAY "EMAIL ME INFO"

"Sure - I'll send the 1-page agreement and the demo video tonight. Quick
heads-up: there are [N] rate-lock spots left in [city]. If you're leaning
yes, just reply YES on the email and I'll hold one for you."
