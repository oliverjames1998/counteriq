# CounterIQ - Paid Pilot Agreement (Outline)

This is an outline. Have a lawyer convert it to your final agreement
before customer #1.

---

**CounterIQ Pilot Program Agreement**

Between **CounterIQ LLC** ("CounterIQ") and **[Owner / LLC]** ("Customer"),
for the location at **[Store Address]**.

## 1. Service tier and pricing
Customer subscribes to the **[Starter / Pro / Advanced]** tier:
- Starter: $299/month, up to 4 cameras
- Pro: $399/month, up to 6 cameras
- Advanced: $499/month, up to 8 cameras

**No setup fee.** Billing begins on install date and recurs monthly.

## 2. First-10 rate lock
Customer is one of the first 10 CounterIQ stores. Customer's monthly rate
is **locked for 12 months** from install date. Standard pricing may rise;
Customer's rate will not change during this period.

If Customer upgrades tiers during the 12-month period, the upgrade rate
is also locked for the remainder of the original 12-month window.

## 3. Term and cancellation
- Initial commitment: **60 days** minimum.
- After day 60, Customer may cancel anytime with **7 days' written
  notice**.
- On cancellation, CounterIQ removes the edge device and either exports
  Customer's data or deletes it, at Customer's choice.

## 4. Equipment
- 1 edge device on loan (included in monthly fee).
- CounterIQ retains ownership.
- Customer returns device within 14 days of cancellation.
- Customer agrees to provide reasonable physical access for install,
  maintenance, and removal.

## 5. Onboarding included
CounterIQ helps Customer configure cameras, draw zones, set business
hours, and complete privacy setup. Standard install is 30-60 minutes.

## 6. Add-ons
Customer may add at any time:
- Extra camera above tier limit: $25-$39/month each
- POS integration: +$99/month
- Optional audio event detection: +$49/month (requires compliance flow)
- Extended clip retention: +$49/month

## 7. Data ownership
- Customer owns all video, audio (if enabled), and event data.
- CounterIQ may use **anonymized, aggregate** data to improve the product.
- CounterIQ will not share specific clips with third parties without
  Customer's written consent.

## 8. Privacy and compliance
- CounterIQ provides templates for customer signage and employee notices.
- Customer is responsible for posting signage and providing employee
  notices in compliance with local law.
- Customer represents and warrants authority to install monitoring on its
  cameras and compliance with applicable recording laws.
- Audio is OFF by default. If Customer enables audio, Customer represents
  it has completed the audio compliance flow and updated signage.

## 9. Liability
- CounterIQ software is **advisory only**. CounterIQ does not produce
  employment, disciplinary, or legal decisions.
- Customer is solely responsible for any action taken on the basis of
  CounterIQ output.
- CounterIQ liability is capped at the greater of **$500** or one month
  of fees paid.

## 10. Intellectual property
- CounterIQ owns all software and infrastructure.
- Customer owns all video and event data.

## 11. Customer commitments
- Bi-weekly 15-minute feedback call (in-person or Zoom) for the first
  60 days.
- If pilot is useful, provide a written or video testimonial by day 90.
- If pilot is recommendable, provide 2 referrals by day 90.

## 12. Confidentiality
- Both parties keep each other's non-public business information
  confidential.
- Customer permits CounterIQ to use **store name and city** in case
  studies and marketing - Customer can opt out by checking a box below.

[ ] I do **not** consent to use of my store name in marketing.

## 13. Payment
- Stripe subscription. First charge on install date.
- Failed payment: 7-day grace + 1 retry, then service paused until paid.

## 14. Governing law
State of Texas.
